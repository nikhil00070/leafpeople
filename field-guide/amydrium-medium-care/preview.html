Field Guide · Amydrium

Amydrium medium and silver: climbing for fenestrations

Amydrium
70% humidity where adult leaves start fenestrating reliably

The quiet Southeast Asian shingler that refuses to mature unless you give it something real to climb.

The picks
01
Amydrium medium
Climber · perforated adult leaves

The species most growers actually want, native from Thailand through the Philippines and Indonesia. Juvenile leaves are small, entire, and easily mistaken for a Rhaphidophora; mature leaves go long, leathery, and split into dramatic windows along the midrib. Give it a rough vertical surface and it will reward you within a season or two. Worth the patience.
