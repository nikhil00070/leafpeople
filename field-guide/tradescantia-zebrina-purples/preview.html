Field Guide · Tradescantia

Tradescantia zebrina and the Easy Purples

Tradescantia
4–6 hrs Direct sun for full pigment

The color is genetic, but the saturation is on you — light, stress, and substrate decide whether your zebrina glows or fades.

The picks
01
Tradescantia zebrina 'Burgundy'
Trailing · broad striped leaf

The benchmark deep form: leaves nearly black-purple on the reverse, with two wide silver bands above that darken to violet in strong light. Holds color far better than generic zebrina when grown bright. Stems thicken quickly and root from any node tossed in water. The cultivar most likely to deliver the saturated look people picture when they say 'wandering dude'.
