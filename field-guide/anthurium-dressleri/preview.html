Anthurium dressleri: Panama's Velvet That Punishes Wet Feet

Anthurium
70% target humidity for stable growth

The blackest velvet in the genus wants chunky bark, a long dry-back, and air moving across its roots — get those right and it forgives almost everything else.

The picks
01
Anthurium dressleri
Terrestrial · velvet leaf

The headline act: matte black-green blades with pale veins, narrow sinus, and a strong geometric posture. Endemic to a small slice of Caribbean-slope Panama, where it grows in leaf litter over rock with constant air movement. In cultivation it sulks in dense peat and rots fast if the crown stays wet. Give it the chunkiest mix you trust and it slowly builds into one of the most arresting plants you'll own.
